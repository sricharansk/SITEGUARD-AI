# Excavation and Trenching Procedure

## Before digging
Underground services must be located using drawings and a cable avoidance tool before any excavation. A permit
to dig is required.

## Protective systems
Excavations 1.2 metres deep or more must be shored, battered back or protected by a trench box before anyone
enters. The protective system must follow the temporary works design.

## Inspection
A competent person must inspect each excavation at the start of every shift and after rain or any event that
could affect stability, and record the inspection.

## Edges
Spoil, materials and plant must be kept at least 1 metre back from the excavation edge. Barriers must be
provided where people or vehicles could fall in.

## Collapse and engulfment
Trench sides can slump, run in or cave in without warning, especially after rain, near spoil heaps, near plant
and in sandy or made ground. Nobody may enter an unsupported trench, pit, manhole dig or foundation excavation
deeper than 1.2 metres; the trench box, sheet piles or shoring must be in place along the whole length where
people work. Tension cracks behind the edge, water seeping in and spoil on the lip are warning signs: get out and
stop work. A person buried or trapped by soil must be dug out with care and taken for medical checks.

## Groundwater and nearby structures
Excavations next to foundations, retaining walls, party walls and roads must follow the temporary works design.
Dewatering and pumping must be planned. Backfilling and compaction next to walls must follow the design to avoid
overloading the wall.
