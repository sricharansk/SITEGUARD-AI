# Excavation and Trenching Procedure

## Before digging
Underground services must be located using drawings and a cable avoidance tool before any excavation. A permit
to dig is required.

## Protective systems
Excavations 1.2 metres deep or more must be shored, battered back or protected by a trench box before anyone
enters. The protective system must follow the temporary works design.

## Inspection
A competent person must inspect each excavation at the start of every shift and after rain or any event that
could affect stability, and record the inspection.

## Edges
Spoil, materials and plant must be kept at least 1 metre back from the excavation edge. Barriers must be
provided where people or vehicles could fall in.
