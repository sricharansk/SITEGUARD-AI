# Regulatory Reference Pointers

## Purpose
This list names public regulations by section so customers can load the official text into Site Guard. It does
not reproduce or interpret the regulations. Compliance decisions must be made by a competent person.

## United States - OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 (Construction)
Subpart M covers fall protection, including duty to have fall protection in 1926.501. Subpart P covers
excavations, including protective systems in 1926.652. Subpart CC covers cranes and derricks in construction.
Subpart K covers electrical. Subpart L covers scaffolds. Subpart E covers personal protective equipment.

## United Kingdom
The Work at Height Regulations 2005, the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015, the Lifting
Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998 (LOLER) and RIDDOR 2013 for incident reporting.

## India
The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act 1996 and
its Central Rules 1998, and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020.
