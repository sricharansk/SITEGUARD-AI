# Inspection and Test Plan - Cast-in-place Concrete

## Hold point 1 - Formwork
Formwork must be inspected for line, level, dimensions, cleanliness and tightness before reinforcement is
fixed. The temporary works coordinator shall sign off formwork before the pour.

## Hold point 2 - Reinforcement
Reinforcement size, spacing, laps and cover must match the approved drawings and bar bending schedule. Cover
spacers must be in place. QA/QC must sign off reinforcement before concrete is placed.

## Concrete placement
Concrete must be placed within the time limit on the delivery ticket and fully compacted with internal
vibrators at a spacing that gives overlapping zones of influence. Slump shall be checked for each truck.

## Testing
Test cubes or cylinders must be taken at the frequency in the specification and tested at 7 and 28 days.
Results below the specified strength shall be reported to the structural engineer within 24 hours.

## Finished surface
Formed surfaces must be free of honeycombing, cold joints and grout loss. Defects must be recorded on a
non-conformance report (NCR) and repaired only by an approved method.

## Honeycombing, voids and blowholes
Honeycombing, voids and exposed aggregate come from poor compaction, grout loss at joints and congested
reinforcement. Each layer must be compacted with a poker vibrator. Blowholes and surface marks on exposed
concrete are a finish defect and are assessed against the specified finish class.

## Cracking, crazing and curing
Plastic shrinkage, early-age thermal and settlement cracks must be recorded with their width and length.
Curing must start as soon as finishing is complete. Crazing in screeds and toppings is assessed by the flooring
contractor and the engineer.

## Cover, spacers and reinforcement checks
Cover is checked with spacers and chairs before the pour and with a covermeter survey after it. Bars sitting on
the shutter, missing or crushed spacers, low cover, wrong bar sizes, missing laps and bent starter bars must be
corrected before the pour or raised on an NCR.

## Delivery tickets, mix and strength
Every delivery ticket must be checked against the specified concrete grade (for example C32/40), slump class and
mix before discharge. Water must not be added on site. Wrong grade, stiff or wet mixes, retarded loads and cube
or cylinder failures are concrete strength non-conformances and must go to the structural engineer.
