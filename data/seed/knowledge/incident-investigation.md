# Incident Reporting and Investigation Procedure

## Reporting
All injuries, near misses, dangerous occurrences and quality non-conformances must be reported in Site Guard
the same shift. The area must be made safe and the scene preserved where it is safe to do so.

## Investigation level
High and critical incidents require a formal investigation led by the HSE manager with a root cause analysis.
Medium incidents require a supervisor investigation. Low incidents require a review.

## Root cause analysis
Investigations shall use the 5-Whys method and consider people, process, equipment, materials, environment and
management factors.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Every action must have an owner, a due date and a verification criterion. Actions for high and critical
incidents must be approved by the HSE manager. An incident shall not be closed until all actions are verified
as effective.
