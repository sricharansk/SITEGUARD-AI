# Lifting Operations Procedure

## Lift plan
Every lift must be covered by a lift plan approved by the appointed person. Complex lifts need a specific plan.

## Rigging
Slings, shackles and lifting accessories must be inspected before use and carry a valid thorough examination
record. Damaged rigging must be quarantined.

## Exclusion zones
No person shall stand or pass under a suspended load. The lift area must be barricaded and controlled by the
slinger/signaller.

## Weather
Lifting must stop when wind speed exceeds the crane manufacturer's limit or the limit in the lift plan,
whichever is lower.

## Crane set-up and ground conditions
Mobile cranes must be set up on ground the lift plan has assessed, with outriggers fully extended on spreader
mats or outrigger pads. Soft ground, tarmac, backfilled trenches and voids under the outriggers can make the
crane overturn or go light. Tower crane and mobile crane lifts must stop if a pad sinks or the crane tilts.

## Lifting accessories and thorough examination
Lifting equipment and accessories (chains, slings, shackles, hooks, lifting beams, lifting anchors and lifting
clutches) need a current LOLER thorough examination. Loads must not exceed the safe working load (SWL). Precast
panels, cladding panels and units must be lifted only by their designed lifting points.

## Controlling the load
The slinger/signaller controls the lift. Tag lines must be used to control swinging loads. People must not barge,
bar or push a load still on the hook or put hands between the load and a fixed structure; lower it and re-rig.
