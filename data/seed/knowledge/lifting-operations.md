# Lifting Operations Procedure

## Lift plan
Every lift must be covered by a lift plan approved by the appointed person. Complex lifts need a specific plan.

## Rigging
Slings, shackles and lifting accessories must be inspected before use and carry a valid thorough examination
record. Damaged rigging must be quarantined.

## Exclusion zones
No person shall stand or pass under a suspended load. The lift area must be barricaded and controlled by the
slinger/signaller.

## Weather
Lifting must stop when wind speed exceeds the crane manufacturer's limit or the limit in the lift plan,
whichever is lower.
