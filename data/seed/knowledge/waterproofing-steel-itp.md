# Inspection and Test Plan - Waterproofing and Structural Steel

## Waterproofing membranes
The substrate must be clean, dry and primed before membrane installation. Laps must meet the manufacturer's
minimum width. A flood test of at least 24 hours is required before screed, finishes or backfill.

## Structural steel connections
Bolts must be tightened to the specified torque and marked after checking. Welds must be visually inspected
and the specified percentage tested by NDT. Welders must hold a current qualification for the procedure used.

## Materials
Only materials with approved submittals and valid mill or batch certificates may be used. Non-conforming
material must be quarantined and tagged.
