# Inspection and Test Plan - Waterproofing and Structural Steel

## Waterproofing membranes
The substrate must be clean, dry and primed before membrane installation. Laps must meet the manufacturer's
minimum width. A flood test of at least 24 hours is required before screed, finishes or backfill.

## Structural steel connections
Bolts must be tightened to the specified torque and marked after checking. Welds must be visually inspected
and the specified percentage tested by NDT. Welders must hold a current qualification for the procedure used.

## Materials
Only materials with approved submittals and valid mill or batch certificates may be used. Non-conforming
material must be quarantined and tagged.

## Leaks, joints and water ingress
Construction joints, kicker joints and pipe penetrations in basements must have a waterstop or hydrophilic strip
as designed. Water coming through a joint, crack or membrane lap, damp patches and leaks from roofs, outlets,
upstands and flashings are recorded as water ingress non-conformances and repaired by an approved method.

## Welds, coatings and NDT
Weld defects include lack of fusion, porosity, undercut, cracks and spatter. Visual inspection, magnetic particle
inspection (MPI) and ultrasonic testing (UT) are recorded per the ITP. Column splices, base plates and bolted
connections must be inspected. Damaged galvanising, paint and intumescent coatings must be repaired.

## Deliveries and goods-in checks
Delivery notes, labels and certificates must be checked at goods-in against the specification, drawings and
schedules: product, grade, size, thickness, colour and supplier. Insulation, rebar, steel, membranes, blocks,
paving and fixings that do not match the specification must be quarantined and not installed.

## Windows, cladding and roofs
Window frames, cills, cavity trays, DPCs, sealant joints and cladding interfaces must be inspected before
internal finishes start. Water leaking round a frame or through a cavity is recorded as water ingress.

## Bolted connections and holes
Bolts must not be left finger tight. Bolt holes must not be flame cut, gas-axed, burnt or reamed oversize to make
steel line up; misaligned connections must be referred to the steel designer.
