# PPE and Temporary Electrical Standard

## Minimum PPE
Safety helmet, safety boots, high-visibility clothing and gloves must be worn in all construction areas. Eye
protection is required for cutting, grinding and drilling.

## Temporary electrical supplies
Temporary supplies must be protected by residual current devices (RCDs) and inspected weekly. Damaged cables
must be removed from use immediately.

## Isolation
Work on or near electrical systems requires isolation, lockout and tagout by an authorised person, and the
circuit must be proved dead before work starts.

## Task-specific PPE
The task risk assessment states the PPE needed beyond the minimum. Safety glasses or goggles are required for
cutting, grinding, drilling and breaking out; a full face shield or visor for grinding and chasing; a welding
visor or helmet with the correct filter shade for arc welding to prevent arc eye. Respiratory protective
equipment (an FFP3 dust mask or respirator, face-fit tested) is required for silica dust, cutting, sanding and
spraying. Hearing protection (ear defenders or plugs) is required in noisy areas. Damaged, cracked or missing PPE
must be replaced before work continues; an operative not wearing the required PPE must stop work. Eyewash
stations must be available where eye injuries are foreseeable.

## Knives, blades and sharp edges
Only safety knives with a retractable or self-retracting blade may be used; open Stanley knives and snap-off
blades are not allowed for cutting membranes, DPM, insulation or plasterboard. Cut-resistant gloves are required
when handling sheet metal, glass, cladding, metal studs and blades. Starter bars and protruding rebar must be
fitted with caps or covers. Protruding nails and screws must be removed or bent over. Cuts and lacerations must
be treated by a first aider and recorded.

## Temporary supplies on site
Site tools and lighting must run at 110 V from a centre-tapped transformer. Extension leads, festoon lighting and
cables must be routed overhead or along walls, kept out of water and protected from damage; daisy-chained leads
are not allowed. Leads with split sheaths, exposed copper or damaged plugs must be withdrawn. Portable tools must
carry a current PAT test label. Distribution boards (DBs) must be locked and labelled.

## Buried and overhead services
Before breaking out, drilling or digging, services must be located with drawings and a CAT and genny scan, and
marked. Overhead power lines must be protected by goal posts and barriers. Striking an LV or HV cable can cause
electric shock, arc flash and burns; stop work, keep clear and report it as a dangerous occurrence.

## Head protection and footwear
Hard hats must be worn wherever there is overhead work, including on decks below crane lifts, scaffolds and
other trades. Safety boots with toe caps and midsole protection are required on rebar decks and all work areas;
trainers and soft shoes are not allowed. Operatives without the right kit are sent to the stores before work.

## Chemicals and coatings
Epoxy, resin, solvent, primer and coating products need a COSHH assessment that names the gloves (for example
nitrile, not cotton) and respiratory filter (organic vapour filters, not dust-only masks) to be used.
