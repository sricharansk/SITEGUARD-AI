# Setting-out, Tolerances and Formwork Striking

## Setting-out
Setting-out of columns, walls and slab edges must be checked by an independent survey before formwork is
closed. The check survey record shall be signed by the site engineer.

## Tolerances
Columns must be plumb within 10 mm per storey and within the tolerances in the specification. Elements out of
tolerance must be recorded on a non-conformance report and referred to the structural engineer.

## Formwork striking and back-propping
Formwork and props must not be removed until the concrete has reached the striking strength confirmed by cube
results or the temporary works design. Early striking requires approval from the temporary works coordinator.

## Crack limits
Crack widths in structural concrete must not exceed 0.3 mm unless the specification states otherwise. Cracks
above the limit must be mapped, monitored and assessed by the structural engineer.
