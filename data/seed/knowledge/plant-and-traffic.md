# Site Traffic and Mobile Plant Rules

## Segregation
Pedestrian routes must be physically separated from plant routes by barriers. Marked crossing points are
required where routes meet.

## Reversing
Plant must not reverse in areas with pedestrians unless a trained banksman is directing the move. Reversing
alarms and cameras must be working before use.

## Operator competence
Only trained and authorised operators may drive plant. Seat belts must be worn where fitted.

## Excavators, dumpers and telehandlers
Excavators must not slew where people can be struck; a slew exclusion zone and banksman are required near
pedestrians. Dumpers must not be overloaded or driven across slopes, edges, starter bars or soft ground where they
can overturn; roll-over protection (ROPS) and seat belts must be used. Telehandlers, forklifts and rollers must
travel with loads low and be operated only by a trained, authorised operator. Keys must be removed when plant is
left.

## Haul roads and blind spots
Haul roads must be maintained, signed and separated from walkways. Drivers have blind spots; a pedestrian struck,
run over or crushed by a vehicle or plant is a critical event. Stop blocks are required where plant tips into
excavations.

## Deliveries
Delivery vehicles, wagons and lorries entering the compound must be met and guided by a banksman, especially when
reversing near welfare cabins, site offices, steps and walkways. Drivers who cannot see must stop.
