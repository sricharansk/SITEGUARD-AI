# Site Traffic and Mobile Plant Rules

## Segregation
Pedestrian routes must be physically separated from plant routes by barriers. Marked crossing points are
required where routes meet.

## Reversing
Plant must not reverse in areas with pedestrians unless a trained banksman is directing the move. Reversing
alarms and cameras must be working before use.

## Operator competence
Only trained and authorised operators may drive plant. Seat belts must be worn where fitted.
