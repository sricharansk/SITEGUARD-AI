## Limits of this evaluation

- The cases are synthetic and labelled by the project team, not by an independent panel of safety and QA/QC professionals. A customer pilot should add expert-labelled cases from its own reports.
- The `dev_v2` and held-out cases were drafted with an AI assistant working separately from the tuning, so they are not real reports and may share phrasing habits with the rules. Before tuning, the summary handed back by the held-out author named the topics of its four BOTH cases and two unclassifiable cases; no rule was written for those topics, but the held-out set is not perfectly blind.
- Scores are for the offline `rules` provider. Run with `SITEGUARD_AI_PROVIDER=anthropic` and an API key to score the Claude provider on the same set.
- Public datasets (OSHA Severe Injury Reports, OSHA ITA case detail) can be scored with `python -m app.datasets.osha_sir` once downloaded; see docs/DATASETS.md.
