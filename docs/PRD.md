# Product Requirements Document

## Product Overview

Site Guard AI is an Agentic AI-powered construction safety and quality decision-support and workflow platform.

## Problem

Construction safety and quality evidence is fragmented across incidents, observations, inspections, documents, images, NCRs and CAPA workflows.

## Primary Goal

Assist teams in moving from incident to evidence-backed investigation, decision support and verified closure.

## Target Users

HSE, Safety, QA/QC, Site Engineering, Project Management, Contractors, Auditors and Clients.

## Core Features

Incident Management, Evidence, RAG, Triage, Safety Investigation, Quality Investigation, Risk, RCA, Compliance, CAPA, Human Approval, Workflow, Vision, Knowledge Graph, Analytics, Reports, Audit and Deployment.

## Non-goals

Autonomous legal certification, autonomous high-impact safety decisions, autonomous regulatory filing.

## MVP

Login, projects, incidents, evidence, RAG, triage, safety/quality investigation, risk, RCA, CAPA, approval, workflow, dashboard and audit.

## Definition of Done

Requirements implemented, tests pass, build passes, security requirements satisfied and docs updated.
