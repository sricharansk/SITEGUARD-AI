## Product should not be positioned as

> “An AI chatbot for construction.”

Multi-agent architecture is an implementation mechanism. Customers care about:

- evidence;
- investigation;
- risk;
- RCA;
- recommended actions;
- accountability;
- verification;
- auditability.

## Product modules

- Incident Management
- Evidence/RAG
- Safety Investigation
- Quality Investigation
- Risk
- RCA
- Compliance
- CAPA
- Human Approval
- Workflow/Escalation
- Vision
- Knowledge Graph
- Analytics
- Audit

## Enterprise positioning statement

> Site Guard AI is an Agentic AI-powered construction safety and quality decision-support and workflow platform that assists HSE, QA/QC, project managers and site teams in investigating incidents, identifying probable root causes, retrieving supporting evidence, assessing risk, recommending corrective and preventive actions, and managing human-approved resolution through verified closure.

The word **assists** is deliberate. The platform should not imply autonomous safety certification or replacement of qualified professionals.
